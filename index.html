Digital Queue Management System

A web-based platform to manage hospital queues digitally, reduce patient waiting time, and ensure priority-based care delivery.

Get Token (Walk-In)

For patients visiting the hospital without an appointment. You will receive a token number and live waiting updates.

Book Appointment

Schedule a consultation for a specific date and time. Recommended for planned visits.

Existing Patient / Sign In

Access your existing appointments or patient details using your registered email.

What is a token?

A token is a number assigned to walk-in patients. It shows your position in the hospital queue and updates live as patients are served.
